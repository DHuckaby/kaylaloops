Kayla Loops

I have way too much yarn, plenty of time, and a serious obsession with crochet. Kayla Loops started with me just “looping” around, and now I can’t stop! I love the thrill of creating something brand new, usually by obsessing over a fresh idea or Googling something fun until I figure out how to make it my own.

Note for custom orders: I don’t keep a library of old patterns, so everything I make is a unique surprise. If you want your own personal “loops,” send me a message and we can chat about a custom project! makemeloops@gmail.com
